Renumbered from 082 because #1426 landed at 083 while this was resolving, so
082 would have been a regression rather than a bump.

Git's line-based merge silently misfiled one field. `enable_office_embedded_image_analysis`
is declared in `office-embedded-image-section` here and in `document-intelligence-section`
on the base; the textual merge put a copy in `shared-conversation-file-approvals-section`,
which is a Workspaces section neither side ever declared it in. Nothing about
that is a conflict, so it merged clean and would have shipped a duplicate
setting under an unrelated group.

Found by diffing field-to-section ownership against both parents rather than
trusting the merge: every key in the result must be declared where at least one
parent declared it, must have exactly one writable owner, and none may vanish.
That check is worth more on this file than the conflict markers are, and it is
the second time it has caught something a clean merge introduced.

The stray copy is removed and the capability keeps its own section. The setting
turns on a separate pass with its own model and its own cost, so filing it under
Document Intelligence implied it was part of that engine's configuration rather
than an independent choice made after it. Recorded in the placement test's
relocation registry alongside the reason, so it reads as a decision.

Also verified base-anchored, per the technique that caught a resurrection
elsewhere in this queue: the merged section set is exactly the base's 58 plus
the 12 this branch adds, `actions-config` stays deleted, and the base section
count is re-derived rather than carried over from the previous sync.
