Content Screening

Check workspace uploads, submitted chat messages, and AI replies using the saved PII, regex, literal, and optional AI rules. Azure AI Content Safety is a separate scanner.

Off by default. Workspace scans require Enhanced Citations and its working private storage under Chat > Citations > Enhanced; chat-only checks do not. Enabling creates an enabled empty baseline if none exists. New uploads use normal processing until applicable checks are added. Disabling new scans or emptying a policy never releases a held document. This master switch and the current upload selection save immediately when you change the master.
