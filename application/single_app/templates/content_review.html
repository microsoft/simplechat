Content review

Review held knowledge before it becomes available to chat, search, or tools.

Only a personal owner or an authorized workspace manager can view protected evidence. Permission to scan all workspaces does not grant access to private findings. Disabling new scans does not release existing holds.

Open Content review from a workspace to fill this automatically.

The candidate is queued or scanning and remains held. This review refreshes automatically until the scan completes.

Canonical evidence is unavailable. Approval and editing require verified evidence; authorized Reject/Delete actions remain available.

Approval requires a completed scan of this exact revision. Missing, pending, or failed checks keep it held.
