Other dependencies
------------------
Cargo.lock is the authoritative, reproducible list of dependency versions and
registry checksums. licenses/DEPENDENCIES.txt lists the locked dependencies,
SPDX license expressions, and source locations. Corresponding upstream license,
copyright and notice files are retained under licenses/dependencies/.
Some dependencies are build-time or platform-specific and are not linked into
every executable. Their notices are retained for both supported target families.

Public regression fixtures
--------------------------
tests/fixtures/SOURCES.txt identifies the exact upstream source paths and Git
blob identities for the included public MPL-2.0 OneNote test files. No files
from the separately licensed AGPL Joplin samples are included. No user-uploaded
notebook, private extraction output, or protected local notebook is distributed.
